The Shape of a Flame

Poetry is silence caught mid-flight, A whisper carved from sound and light. It bends the rules, it breaks the day, It paints what reason can't convey. It is the soul's soft, secret tongue, A fire where quiet hearts are hung. It weeps in ink, it laughs in rhyme, It folds the ache of space and time. Not just the sweet, but bitter too, It bleeds what many never do. A mirror held to joy or pain, A storm poured into calm refrain. So when the world forgets to feel, And hearts grow numb, and wounds won't heal A poem dares to still remain, The ghost of thought, the shape of flame.

In His Being

He is the Word before words had breath, the Light no darkness has ever caught. Unmoved, He moves; unchanged, He shapes the turning of every fleeting thought. He needs no throne, yet owns them all His rest is louder than our striving. He empties self to fill the low, and calls it glory, this self-giving. He hides in bread, in wine, in dust, in the quiet weeping no one sees. The universe hangs on His word, yet He kneels down to wash our feet.
